Updated: October 7, 2026

This page explains the app and its purchases. Apps obtained through the App Store are licensed under the Apple Standard End User License Agreement (EULA). These explanations do not limit your statutory consumer rights.

Basic use

The app is free to download. Basic bookkeeping requires no Shiyu account or sign-in and includes local entries, ledgers, categories, tags, reports and budgets. Simplified Chinese and English are supported. Changing language changes the interface and display format, not stored amounts or user content. Ledgers use Chinese yuan (CNY); changing language does not convert currency.

In-app purchases

The app offers optional in-app purchases. Pro includes personal iCloud sync, creating and maintaining shared ledgers, CSV and complete backup export, screenshot recognition, and passcode and Face ID unlock. A shared ledger is covered by its creator's active Pro. Invitees do not need a separate purchase for that shared ledger, but do not receive personal Pro features. iCloud+ storage is not included.

  • Free 7-day trial: claimed through the App Store once per App Store account, lasting 168 hours from the original claim. It does not charge or become a subscription when it ends. Reinstallation or restoring purchase information does not restart it.
  • Annual Pro: reference price in mainland China is CNY 16 per year, as an auto-renewable subscription.
  • Lifetime Pro: reference price in mainland China is CNY 30, as a one-time purchase with no renewal.

Available products, currency and prices are shown on the App Store purchase confirmation screen. Apple processes payment. Annual subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends; Apple may charge for renewal within the 24 hours before expiration. Manage or cancel through Apple subscription settings. Canceling renewal keeps access through the paid period. Uninstalling the app or buying Lifetime Pro does not cancel an existing annual subscription. Request refunds through Apple Report a Problem, subject to Apple's decision and applicable law.

Expiration and data

Trial or subscription expiration does not delete your ledgers or history. Basic entries, import and restore remain available. Features requiring active Pro, including sync, export, screenshot recognition and creating shares, pause. An enabled app lock remains active and can be changed or disabled after passcode verification. Keep copies of important data and review the confirmation before deleting a ledger.

Sharing, recognition and other services

iCloud sync and sharing require iCloud sign-in on your device and are subject to Apple's terms. Authorized members can access the shared ledger according to their permissions; your other private ledgers remain separate. Copies already exported or copied by members cannot be recalled. Send invitations only to trusted people.

On-device OCR works offline. Optional Doubao AI shortcuts require Doubao installation, sign-in and internet access, and send screenshots and recognition prompts to Doubao under its own terms and privacy policy. Recognition can be wrong: review amounts, dates and categories before confirming. Shiyu does not automatically retrieve transactions from banks or provide investment, tax or financial advice.
