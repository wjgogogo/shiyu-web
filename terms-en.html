Updated: October 11, 2026

Welcome to Shiyu Ledger. Applications obtained through the Apple App Store are licensed under the Apple Standard End User License Agreement (EULA). These Terms further explain the scope of services and purchase terms.

1. Scope of Service & Core Features

Shiyu Ledger is an offline-first personal and family expense tracking tool. The free base version provides local transaction recording, multiple ledgers, custom categories, tags, reports, and monthly budgets. Basic bookkeeping requires no accounts or registration, with all data saved locally on your device.

2. Pro Plans & Purchase Rules

The app offers optional Pro premium capabilities, including personal iCloud multi-device sync, family shared ledger collaboration, smart screenshot recognition, data export, and passcode/biometric protection:

  • 7-Day Free Trial: Available once per Apple ID (valid for 168 hours). The trial terminates automatically without unexpected charges or subscriptions.
  • Annual Pro (Auto-Renewable): Reference price in mainland China is CNY 16/year. Renews automatically annually unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing cycle.
  • Lifetime Pro (One-Time Purchase): Reference price in mainland China is CNY 30. A single payment unlocking permanent Pro access without renewals.
  • Family Sharing Coverage: A shared ledger is covered by the creator's active Pro subscription. Invited members join that shared ledger without needing separate Pro purchases. Private ledgers remain independent.

Actual prices and currency are displayed on the App Store confirmation screen. Payments and billing are processed directly by Apple.

3. Subscription Management & Refunds

You may manage or cancel auto-renewal at any time in iOS "Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions". To request a refund, please submit an application via Apple Report a Problem. Refunds are determined by Apple's policies and applicable consumer protection laws. Uninstalling the app does not automatically cancel an active subscription.

4. Membership Expiry & Data Preservation

When trial or subscription access expires, your transaction history and ledgers are never deleted. Basic tracking, viewing, data import, and local backup restoration remain accessible. Features requiring active entitlements (cloud sync, advanced export, OCR) pause.

5. Screenshot Recognition & Disclaimer

On-device OCR processes images locally and offline. Optional Shortcuts integrations forward screenshots to third-party services only upon your explicit action. Recognition drafts require your verification before saving. Shiyu Ledger is a personal expense tracking tool and does not connect to banks for automatic debiting or offer investment, tax, or financial advisory services.
