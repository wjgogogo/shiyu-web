FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use the app without creating an account?

Yes. Core bookkeeping works completely offline without registering or logging in. An Apple account on your device is only required when enabling iCloud sync or family sharing.

Where is my financial data stored?

All entries are stored locally on your device by default. When sync is enabled, data is stored in your private Apple CloudKit container. We operate no servers that store your financial records. See our Privacy Policy.

Will I be automatically charged after the 7-day trial?

No. The 7-day trial is a standalone trial period. It terminates automatically without charging or converting into a subscription. Auto-renewal only applies if you actively purchase the Annual plan.
