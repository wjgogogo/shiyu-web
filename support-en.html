User Guide & Technical Support

A clean, offline-first personal finance tracker. For inquiries or assistance, contact support at 906626481@qq.com.

1. Core Tracking & Privacy

Core bookkeeping is completely free and requires no account. Transactions, categories, tags, and notes are saved locally on your device by default, working fully offline. We do not inspect your banking passwords or SMS messages, and include zero third-party tracking SDKs.

2. Home & Lock Screen Widgets

  • Home Screen Widgets: Quick entry shortcuts, income/expense summaries, and remaining budget progress.
  • Lock Screen Widgets: One-tap entry, daily expense totals, and monthly spending trends.
  • How to Add: Touch and hold your Home Screen or customize your Lock Screen, search for "Shiyu", and choose your preferred default ledger and categories.

3. Screenshot Recognition

  • On-Device OCR: Extracts transaction amounts and merchant names locally using iOS native text recognition without internet connection.
  • Smart Shortcuts Integration: Trigger via "Me → Shortcuts" to parse receipt screenshots using automated workflows.
  • Review Before Saving: Recognition results are presented as drafts for your confirmation before writing to the ledger.

4. Family Sharing & iCloud Sync

  • Personal iCloud Sync: Automatically merges and syncs data across your Apple devices using Apple CloudKit.
  • Shared Family Ledgers: A creator with an active Pro subscription can invite family members. Invitees join for free with their personal iCloud accounts without needing Pro.
  • Strict Isolation: Only the selected shared ledger is accessible to members; personal ledgers remain strictly confidential.

5. Pro Plans & Pricing Rules

  • 7-Day Free Trial: Available once per Apple ID. Automatically terminates when expired, with no charges or auto-renewals.
  • Annual Pro (CNY 16/year): Auto-renewing annual subscription, manageable anytime in iOS Settings.
  • Lifetime Pro (CNY 30): One-time payment unlocking permanent Pro access.
  • Data Preservation: All historical transactions and ledgers remain fully accessible if Pro access ends. Basic tracking, import, and backup restore are always free.

6. Subscriptions & Refunds

  • Restore Purchases: Sign in with the purchasing Apple ID and tap "Restore Purchases" in settings.
  • Manage Subscriptions: Go to iPhone "Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions" to manage or cancel auto-renewal.
  • Request Refunds: In-app purchases are handled by Apple. To request a refund, visit the Apple Report a Problem page.

7. Data Backup & Restore

Export your ledger into standard CSV spreadsheets or complete backup files anytime. Restoring backups is free forever. We recommend periodically exporting copies to local Files or iCloud Drive via "Settings → Data Backup".

8. Problem Reporting

When contacting support, please include your iOS version, app version, reproduction steps, and screenshots. Please mask sensitive personal details such as passwords or full account numbers before sending.
