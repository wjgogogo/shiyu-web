Ad-free. No Shiyu login.

Basic bookkeeping is free and works offline, with no Shiyu account required. Ledgers, categories, tags and notes stay on your device first. Optional cloud features require iCloud sign-in.

Screenshot recognition

Pro includes on-device OCR and optional Doubao AI shortcuts. OCR works offline. Doubao requires installation, sign-in, internet and permission to process screenshots and prompts. Results return to Shiyu for your review before saving. Find the shortcuts in Me → Shortcuts, then optionally assign Back Tap or your iPhone's Action button.

Home & Lock Screen widgets

Add quick entry, income and expense totals, remaining budget and expense trend widgets. Touch and hold your Home Screen or customize your Lock Screen, find Shiyu, then choose a ledger and favorite categories.

Free features and in-app purchases

  • Basic bookkeeping, reports, budgets, import and restore are free.
  • Try Pro free for 7 days, once per App Store account. No charge or automatic subscription at the end.
  • Annual Pro: CNY 16 per year in mainland China, renewing automatically after you choose to purchase.
  • Lifetime Pro: CNY 30 in mainland China, paid once. No monthly plan.

The App Store confirmation screen shows the actual price and available products. Pro includes personal iCloud sync, creating and maintaining shared ledgers, screenshot recognition, export and app lock. iCloud+ storage is not included. A creator's active Pro covers invited shared-ledger members, but does not unlock their personal Pro features.

Purchases and subscriptions

Use the same App Store account as your purchase. The app automatically rechecks access when opened. Trial time remains based on the first claim. Manage annual renewal in Apple subscription settings and request refunds through Apple Report a Problem. Uninstalling the app or purchasing Lifetime Pro does not cancel an existing annual subscription.

Language and amounts

Choose System default, 简体中文 or English in Me → App settings → Language. English uses three-digit grouping, for example ¥1,234,567.89. Chinese keeps four-digit grouping. Stored amounts, currency and your own ledger names and notes do not change.

Your data and feedback

See the privacy controls for sync, sharing and deletion. If reporting a problem, include the app and iOS versions, steps and error message. Redact unrelated names, amounts and account details in screenshots. Never send passwords or verification codes.
