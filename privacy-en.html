Privacy Policy

Updated: October 11, 2026

Shiyu Ledger ("we", "our") values your personal privacy and data security. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle, protect, and respect your information when you use our application.

1. Local Storage & Offline Tracking

Transactions, accounts, categories, tags, budgets, notes, and custom avatars entered in the app are stored locally on your device by default. Basic bookkeeping requires no account registration. The app does not request banking passwords or automatically inspect background payment transactions.

2. iCloud Sync & Family Sharing

Multi-device sync and shared family ledgers are built upon Apple CloudKit infrastructure:

  • Transmission & Storage: When you actively enable cloud sync or sharing, data is encrypted and transferred to your private Apple iCloud container, subject to the Apple Privacy Policy.
  • Scope of Sharing: Sharing applies solely to the specific ledger you select. Private ledgers remain strictly isolated and inaccessible to other members.
  • No Custom Servers: We operate no custom business servers storing your financial records.

3. Device Permissions

The app requests system permissions only when you explicitly use specific features:

  • Photos & Camera: Used only when setting avatars, importing receipt screenshots, or scanning category sharing QR codes.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: Used exclusively for local app lock authentication. Biometric credentials remain stored in your device's Secure Enclave and are never accessible to the app.

4. Smart OCR & Shortcuts

On-device OCR text recognition runs locally and offline without transmitting images externally. If you choose optional Shortcuts integrations, screenshots are processed only upon your explicit action under third-party privacy policies. Draft results return to the device for your review before saving.

5. Advertising, Tracking & Analytics

Shiyu Ledger contains zero third-party advertising SDKs or tracking analytics libraries. We do not collect IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers), conduct cross-app tracking, or sell/share financial data with third parties.

6. Data Modification & Deletion

You have full authority to view, modify, or permanently delete entries and ledgers within the app. Deleting a local ledger clears associated local records; dissolving a shared ledger destroys the corresponding cloud zone. You can export complete backups or clear all data at any time.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or personal data safety, please email us at: 906626481@qq.com. Please do not include account passwords or sensitive financial credentials in support messages.
