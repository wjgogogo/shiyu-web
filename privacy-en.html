Privacy Policy

Updated: October 7, 2026

This policy explains how the iOS app handles your data.

Local bookkeeping and profile

Your income, expenses, accounts, categories, tags, budgets, notes, nickname and avatar are primarily stored on your device. Basic bookkeeping needs no Shiyu account. The app does not read bank passwords or automatically retrieve transaction history from banks or payment platforms.

Optional iCloud sync and sharing

Personal sync and shared ledgers use Apple CloudKit and require iCloud sign-in. When enabled, the relevant ledger and member identification information are transmitted to and stored in Apple's cloud services, subject to Apple's terms and privacy policy. Cloud identity and the creator's Pro entitlement are used to provide access and sharing.

Authorized members can view shared entries, configuration and member profiles according to their permissions. Only the selected ledger is shared; other private ledgers remain separate. Creators can stop sharing and invitees can leave. This cannot recall content members have already copied or exported. Pro does not include iCloud+ storage.

Photos, camera and screenshots

System photo and camera capabilities are used only when you choose images, set an avatar, import screenshots, scan category QR codes or save sharing cards. OCR and QR recognition run on your device, without sending images to a developer-operated server. Avatars may sync with sharing you enable. Confirmed entries follow their ledger's sync settings. Manage camera and photo permissions in iOS Settings.

Optional Doubao AI

On-device OCR works without internet. If you install and run a Doubao bookkeeping shortcut, screenshots, recognized text and prompts containing the current ledger and category configuration are sent to Doubao. This requires internet, the Doubao app and sign-in. Review the system permission prompts and Doubao's own service terms and privacy policy before use. Choose local OCR or manual entry if you do not want to use this third-party AI service.

Results return to Shiyu for review and are only saved after your confirmation. Check amounts, dates, types and categories. This process does not upload screenshots or ledgers to a developer-operated server.

Purchases and trial

Apple handles purchases, refunds and subscriptions. The app reads StoreKit-verified product, transaction and entitlement information to enable Pro and recheck purchased access. The separate 7-day free trial is measured from the original claim and does not charge when it ends. Annual and lifetime purchases are optional. The developer does not receive your payment card details or payment password. A shared ledger creator's entitlement expiry is used to determine whether shared Pro features remain active.

Import, export and support

You choose which files to import, what to export, and recipients or locations when using the system share sheet. Exported records can contain personal information. If you email support, the developer receives your email address and the information you choose to send, to respond to and handle your request. Do not send passwords, verification codes, identity documents or unnecessary complete ledgers. Support messages are retained only as needed to resolve the request or meet applicable legal obligations; you can ask for deletion.

Control and deletion

You can view, edit and delete entries, delete ledgers, and manage sharing permissions in the app. Deleting a cloud ledger you own removes its app-managed cloud record zone and local ledger after cloud confirmation. For an invited ledger, leaving removes your access; deleting its local copy does not delete the creator's ledger. Copies already downloaded or exported by other members cannot be remotely erased.

Deleting entries, disabling sync, leaving a share and canceling a subscription are separate actions. Uninstalling the app does not by itself remove iCloud data or Apple's purchase records. The developer cannot directly read your device or private iCloud ledger. Contact support for assistance with data controls. Pro expiration keeps existing data and basic bookkeeping available while pausing features that require an active entitlement.

Advertising, tracking and other services

The app has no advertising or third-party behavioral analytics SDK, does not use advertising identifiers for cross-app tracking, and does not sell ledger data to advertisers. Apple's services are governed by the Apple Privacy Policy. Opening external support or policy pages uses your browser and the hosting provider's normal web delivery; this does not upload your ledger.

Children, updates and contact

Minors should use the app with a guardian's guidance and obtain permission before purchases. We update this policy when features or data practices change. For privacy, access or deletion questions, contact 906626481@qq.com.
